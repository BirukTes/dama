# Difficulty and accessibility polish

Help now describes the selected challenge setting and distinguishes the exact puzzle defender. Gentle uses two-ply lookahead and a 25% chance of choosing another tactically safe move. Standard keeps the existing Coral/Runner/Guardian depths of 3/4/6 and the shared 24,000-node decision budget. All rivals still take an immediate win and avoid an immediate losing reply when possible. No heuristic weights were changed on the strength of a tiny automated sample.

The search now caches nonterminal static evaluations per decision. The cache includes the board/map, deliveries and turn; terminal wins/draws bypass it because their value depends on history and turn count. Comparing 288 decisions across six maps, three rivals and two settings against the previous implementation produced identical choices. The cache is discarded after each decision and cannot grow beyond that bounded search's positions.

`npm.cmd run analyze:maps` runs a repeatable 72-game sample across all six maps, all three rivals and both settings, alternating starters. Its Blue opponent is an independent one-ply distance/delivery policy with 20% seeded random moves. The report includes rival W/L/D, match length and PC decision timing. See [the recorded sample](calibration-sample.json). Two games per combination are regression evidence only: they cannot establish a human difficulty ranking or prove Standard wins more often. Crossroads and Long Crossing draws are useful playtest targets; Guardian's wider-board timing also needs phone measurement. Keep the larger Classic reference comparison in `analyze:rivals` for its original purpose.

The game board now uses one Tab stop with arrow navigation between spots. Enter/Space retain native button selection and movement. Escape clears a selected pawn; a subsequent Escape opens Pause. Native dialog Escape closes it and returns focus. Help documents these controls, and Settings/restart dialogs have explicit accessible titles. Puzzle previews show the real graph, completion uses text/checkmarks as well as colour, and new pack controls meet a 44px minimum touch height. Compact layouts preserve the Home view at tested 320×568 and 844×390 sizes, including a saved puzzle.

Automated coverage includes keyboard selection/Escape/focus, theme/difficulty help, all 21 puzzle solutions, map-aware saves, backups, independent journeys and optional achievements. Physical iPhone/Android installation, screen-reader output, sound interruption, safe-area fit, touch comfort and perceived difficulty still require real-device/user playtesting.
